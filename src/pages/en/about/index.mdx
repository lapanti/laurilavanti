    <p class="answer" slot="answer">I am the initiator and primary contact of the Digital Independence citizen initiative. Our initiative calls for legislation ensuring that critical public digital services and data remain in the hands of Finnish and European actors, on servers located in the EU/EEA area.</p>
    <p class="detail" slot="detail">Digital independence supports European technological development, reduces dependence on individual vendors, and creates better conditions for domestic software development. It is not an ideology but a practical prerequisite for a functioning society — and it also strengthens democracy and citizens' rights, while creating markets for new business activity.</p>
</Pillar>
