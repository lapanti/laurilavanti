# Design System: Lavanti.fi

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Signal Band"**

Five colors run through this system the way a broadcast carrier runs through a transmission — pine green, vivid green, sky aqua, bright sky, signal blue. They are the constant. Everything else is built to carry them: flat plates of color with no gradient and no shadow, poster-scale uppercase display type, and solid rules where a softer system would use a border-radius or a drop shadow. The band is not decoration applied to the page; it is the page's organizing idea, and the layout exists to give it somewhere to run.

The register is civic and declarative. This reads as a public notice rather than a campaign pitch — plain, confident, unhurried, nothing pleading for attention. Authority comes from the craft of the printing, not from gloss. That register is doing strategic work: it carries the rule-of-law tone one part of the audience needs while holding the professional credibility another part requires, without either group having to read past a movement aesthetic.

Density is deliberately uneven. Display type is enormous (120px at desktop, still 48px at 375px) and sits against generous vertical rhythm, while body copy stays at a comfortable 17px on a 1152px measure. The jump between those two scales *is* the hierarchy — there is no intermediate ladder of sizes doing the work, and no shadow or elevation cue to fall back on. Color plates segment the page; rules divide within a plate.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Five signal colors as the system's constant, free in arrangement
- Absolutely flat: no shadows, no gradients, no rounded corners anywhere
- Poster-scale uppercase display type against small, steady body copy
- Solid rules (2–5px) as the only divider vocabulary
- Full-bleed color plates as the unit of page structure
- Accent plates each carry their own matched ink pair

## Colors

A warm, printed palette: sand grounds, three soft accent plates, and five saturated signal colors that never appear as large fields of flat background behind body copy.

### Primary
- **Deep Forest** (#163E35): The system's ink and its darkest ground. Page background, hero left panel, footer, all display headings on light plates, solid button fills, and input borders. The single most load-bearing color.
- **Pine Green** (#006845): The active/interactive green. Link rules, eyebrows on sand plates, focus outlines on light ground, button hover fill, post titles, and the mobile menu's bottom border. First band of the signal sequence.
- **Vivid Green** (#009639): The emphasis green, reserved for the `<em>` inside the hero headline and text selection. Second band.

### Named Rules

**The Signal Band Rule.** The five signal colors — pine green, vivid green, sky aqua, bright sky, signal blue — are a fixed palette, not a fixed object. Their arrangement is free: a full-bleed horizontal band, a vertical column at a plate's edge, angled, subdivided into fine repeating lines, or used as texture. What must not change is the set itself. Reach for a sixth color and the band stops being recognizable.

**The Matched Ink Rule.** Every accent plate carries its own ink pair — a near-black for headings and body, a mid-tone for eyebrows — drawn from the plate's own hue. Never set a plate's text in the global text colors. Oat takes #2A2A18 / #625E37, peach takes #33251A / #6E5845, sky aqua takes #12312C / #466661.

**The Signal-Is-Not-A-Ground Rule.** The five signal colors appear as bands, marks, and small emphasis. They are never the background behind a paragraph of body copy. Grounds come from the sand, off-white, light-gray, and accent-plate families only.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Big Shoulders Display (with Haettenschweiler, Arial Narrow, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** IBM Plex Sans (with Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif)

**Character:** A tall, narrow, high-contrast condensed face set at poster scale against a humanist sans that is quiet to the point of self-effacement. The pairing carries the whole tonal argument: the display face is the public notice, the body face is the person explaining it.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (900, 120px, line-height 1, -0.02em, uppercase): Hero headline and top-level section headings. Steps down to 88px at 1199px, 72px at 999px, 64px at 768px, 52px at 380px. Pillar headings run slightly smaller (108px, -0.015em); the synthesis heading runs 110px.
- **Headline** (900, 58px, line-height 1, -0.015em, uppercase): Second-tier section headings — biography, writings, newsletter. Drops to 48px on mobile, 42px for the newsletter.
- **Title** (600, 19px, line-height 1.3): Set in the *body* face, not the display face. FAQ questions (19px) and post titles (18px). The deliberate break to IBM Plex Sans is what keeps a dense question list readable.
- **Body** (400, 17px, line-height 1.68): Default copy on a 1152px measure; the lede runs 19px on a 548px max-width. Small copy is 15px, fine print 13px.
- **Label** (700, 15px, letter-spacing 0.176em, uppercase): Eyebrows, in the display face. Related tracked-uppercase roles: navigation 18px/0.055em, buttons 21px/0.07em, link rules 14px/0.12em, chips 11px/0.13em, post dates 14px/0.137em, footer headings 15px/0.173em.

### Named Rules

**The Two-Scale Rule.** Hierarchy comes from the gap between poster-scale display type and steady 15–19px body copy, not from a ladder of intermediate sizes. If a heading needs to sit between 58px and 17px, the problem is the layout, not the type scale.

**The Open Leading Rule.** Big Shoulders is set at line-height 1 and needs it opened to roughly 0.95–0.97 in multi-line uppercase Finnish — Ä and Ö collide with the line above at tighter settings. Verified across directions 06–17.

**The Tracked Uppercase Rule.** Every uppercase run in the display face carries positive letter-spacing, scaled inversely to size: 0.055em at 18px, 0.176em at 15px, 0.13em at 11px. Untracked uppercase in this face is unreadable at small sizes.

## Layout

A single centered column system. The shell is full-width with a 1152px measure (`--measure`) centered inside it, and a horizontal gutter (`--pad-x`) that steps 56px → 40px → 24px → 16px as the viewport narrows. Vertical rhythm is one section token (`--sec-y`) of 88px, dropping to 60px on mobile; several sections override to 96px.

Page structure is **plates**: full-bleed bands of a single flat color stacked vertically, each holding a centered measure. The plate is the unit of composition — sections are distinguished by ground color, not by cards or containers.

The hero is the one asymmetric composition: a fixed 470px image panel beside a fluid content panel, at a minimum height of `clamp(640px, 92svh, 899px)`. The image column narrows to 380px at 1199px and 330px at 999px, then collapses at 768px into a 222px image band above stacked copy.

Internal grids are plain and few: two columns for pillar bodies, biography, FAQ, and the newsletter; three for post cards; four for credentials and footer links. Everything halves at 1199px and goes single-column at 768px.

Breakpoints: 1199px (tablet), 999px (narrow tablet), 768px (mobile, matching the 390px Figma frame), 380px (small phone).

### Named Rules

**The Plate Rule.** A new ground color means a new full-bleed plate, edge to edge. Never inset a colored panel inside a plate with margin around it — that reads as a card, and this system has no cards.

## Elevation & Depth

**This system has no shadows.** Not a subtle one, not an ambient one. There is no `box-shadow` anywhere in the implementation, and adding one breaks the printed logic the whole system rests on.

Depth is conveyed three ways instead: **flat tonal plates** (a lighter or cooler ground marks a new movement), **solid rules** (2–5px lines in deep forest, sand, or a plate's own ink), and **overlap** (the synthesis section's lion sits behind the text on z-index 1, its vertical stripe column behind that). Layering is explicit and orthogonal, never simulated with blur.

### Named Rules

**The No-Shadow Rule.** Depth comes from color, rule, and overlap. If an element needs to feel separated from its ground, change the ground or draw a rule — never lift it.

## Shapes

**Zero radius everywhere.** Corners are square without exception, and the email input carries an explicit `border-radius: 0` to defeat the mobile browser default. There is no radius token beyond `none`.

The form language is entirely **rules and rectangles**. Borders are solid and deliberately heavy, at four established weights: 2px (button outlines, navigation underlines, footer colophon divider), 3px (FAQ dividers, focus outlines, email input, pillar read-list rules), 4px (credential markers, mobile menu base), and 5px (post card rules). Rules mark relationships between things; they never enclose anything — there are no boxed containers in the system.

The one recurring non-rectangular form is the photograph, always full-bleed inside its frame with a fixed aspect ratio (564/392 for the biography portrait) and a considered `object-position`.

### Named Rules

**The Rule-Not-Box Rule.** A line above a thing groups it. A line around a thing boxes it. This system only does the first.

## Components

### Buttons

Interactive elements feel **tactile and confident**: flat and printed at rest, then answering a press with physical displacement rather than a color-swap alone. Both parts ship in `00-home-final.html`.

- **Shape:** Square (0 radius), 2px solid border in `currentColor`.
- **Outline (primary):** Deep forest text on the plate's own ground, no fill at rest, 16px 28px padding, display face at 21px/0.07em uppercase. A pine-green variant swaps the text color.
- **Hover:** Inverts. Fill becomes deep forest, text becomes light sand (or pine green → off-white for the green variant), over 180ms on `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`.
- **Pressed:** `translate(2px, 2px)` over 120ms on the same curve, held in the `--press` token. The element moves as one piece; nothing is left behind and no rule thickens.
- **Solid:** Deep forest fill with oat-yellow text, no border, 15.2px 32px padding, used for the newsletter submit. Hover fills pine green, and it takes the same 2px press.
- **Mobile:** Drops to 18px type and 12px 20px padding. The press offset is unchanged, and is the only interaction feedback available on touch.

### Named Rules

**The Press Rule.** Anything that can be pressed moves 2px down and right while held, scaled to the element's weight: 2px for buttons, 1px for the link rule. Structural elements never move — navigation, inputs, and vocabulary chips stay fixed, because a moving nav feels unstable and a chip that responds promises an action it does not have. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the offset is dropped and the color inversion carries the whole interaction.

### Chips
- **Style:** Pillar vocabulary tags. Deep forest at 14% opacity over the plate's own ground, plate ink text, square, 4.48px 8.8px padding, display face at 11px/0.13em uppercase.
- **State:** Static — these are labels, not filters. No hover, no selected state.

### Cards / Containers

There are no cards. The nearest equivalent is the **post entry**: a 5px sand rule across the top, a 184px cropped image, a tracked date, a title in the body face at 600, and a 15px excerpt — all on the plate's own ground with no border, background, or padding of its own.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 3px solid deep forest border, transparent background, square, 14.4px 16px padding, 17px body face, pine-green caret. Placeholder in oat ink soft.
- **Focus:** The global treatment — 3px pine-green outline at 3px offset. Inside a dark region the outline switches to oat yellow via `.on-dark`.
- **Press:** None. A field is not pressed, so it does not move (see The Press Rule).
- **Error / Disabled:** Not yet defined.

### Navigation
- **Desktop:** Absolutely positioned over the hero, 68px tall, transparent. Links in the display face at 18px/0.055em uppercase, deep forest on a light-sand background chip, with a transparent 2px bottom border that becomes pine green on hover. Language switcher to the right at 15px/0.12em; the active language takes a sand background.
- **Mobile (≤768px):** Becomes a fixed 60px solid deep-forest bar with the white wordmark and a 44px hamburger. The panel drops below the bar on a deep-forest ground with a 4px pine-green base rule; links go oat yellow at 26px, the active language takes a pine-green chip with white text.

### The Stripe

The system's signature component and the source of its North Star. Five equal flex children, one per signal color, filling their container.

- **Horizontal:** 220px tall at desktop, 100px at mobile, full-bleed, used as the rule between major movements. Sequence runs pine → vivid → aqua → sky → blue.
- **Vertical:** 305px wide at the synthesis section's right edge (180px at 1199px, hidden at 768px), with the sequence **deliberately reversed** — blue → sky → aqua → vivid → pine.
- Per the Signal Band Rule, other arrangements are open; these two are what the home page uses.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** treat the five signal colors as an inseparable set, however they are arranged.
- **Do** give every accent plate its own matched ink pair rather than the global text colors.
- **Do** build page structure from full-bleed plates of flat color.
- **Do** track uppercase display type positively, scaled inversely to size (0.055em at 18px → 0.13em at 11px).
- **Do** open Big Shoulders' leading toward 0.95–0.97 for multi-line uppercase Finnish, so Ä and Ö clear the line above.
- **Do** state depth with ground color, solid rules, and explicit overlap.
- **Do** keep every corner square, including an explicit `border-radius: 0` on inputs.
- **Do** verify contrast rather than assuming it; AA on all text/background pairs is the established floor.
- **Do** let `prefers-reduced-motion` disable all motion, and make every reveal degrade to visible without JavaScript.
- **Do** give every pressable element the 2px press offset (1px for the link rule), and withhold it from anything structural.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a shadow, gradient, glow, or blur anywhere in this system.
- **Don't** round a corner.
- **Don't** let it read as a SaaS marketing site — no glassmorphism, no soft-shadowed rounded cards, no purple-blue accent, no floating UI screenshots.
- **Don't** set body copy on a saturated signal color as its background.
- **Don't** box an element with a border; use a rule above it instead.
- **Don't** introduce a sixth brand color, or a third typeface beyond the two here.
- **Don't** fill the gap between 58px and 19px with new intermediate heading sizes.
- **Don't** leave a rule, outline, or ghost behind when an element is pressed; the offset shadow is not this system's idea of depth.
