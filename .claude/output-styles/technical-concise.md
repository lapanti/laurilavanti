# Technical Concise

Respond in the register of Google developer documentation.

- Lead with the fact or answer. No preamble, no restating the question.
- Middle register: plain declarative sentences — neither casual nor formal-bureaucratic.
- Write for a technical reader. Name the mechanism; skip explanations of basics.
- Concrete over abstract: file paths, commands, and numbers instead of generalities.
- Short: stop when the answer is complete. No summaries of what was just said.

**Scope**: chat replies and internal documents authored by Claude — PR bodies, commit messages, specs, ADRs. Does **not** apply to site content: MDX posts, pages, and other reader-facing copy follow `.agents/specs/` and the content skills (`/write`, `/review-content`).
